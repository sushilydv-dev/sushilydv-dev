### Featured project

#### [Airglide](https://github.com/sushilydv-dev/Airglide): present hands-free

A gesture-controlled presentation app for macOS and Windows. Your webcam becomes the remote: change slides, point with a laser, draw, and move the mouse with hand gestures, with an AI assistant that runs entirely on your computer.

- Real-time hand tracking with on-device gesture recognition, debouncing and per-user calibration
- Works with PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides, LibreOffice Impress and PDFs
- Offline AI slide assistant, plus automated macOS and Windows builds and releases with GitHub Actions

`Python` `OpenCV` `MediaPipe` `llama.cpp` `pywebview` `PyInstaller` `GitHub Actions`

Website: **[Airglide-web](https://github.com/sushilydv-dev/Airglide-web)**: `React` `Vite` `Tailwind CSS` `Motion` `Vercel`
